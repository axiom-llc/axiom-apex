# APEX commercial opportunity intake — 2026-11-05

**Status:** proposed B2B use cases; no new deployed feature or external authority. Global economic rank and buyer gates are owned by the private Director record `operator/revenue-module-portfolio-2026-11-05.md`; APEX owns the actual runtime contracts and their tests.

## Reuse-first foundation

APEX already has schema-validated plan execution, conservative crash recovery/effect ledger, ASON authorization integration, and independent bounded subprocess batches via `apex swarm --tasks tasks.json --workers 4 --human-loop`. The swarm does **not** have implemented inter-agent communication, shared planning or swarm-wide recovery. Do not replace working implementations, add a second orchestrator, or confuse CLI demonstrations with commercial production readiness.

## Validation and risk

1. **No-build first:** establish economic buyer, paying precedent, incumbent/freely available alternative, acceptance test, source-data rights and founder advantage.
2. **Benchmarks:** compare validated task completion, exception and recovery rates against the existing APEX baseline; do not claim speedup just because workers are parallel.
3. **External effects:** require explicit Owner/customer approval for outreach, contracts, paid API use, production configuration, financial transactions, intrusive testing or deployment. Do not invent payment outcomes.
4. **Non-goals:** no 10,000-agent architecture, no unbounded arbitrary-code execution, no new scheduler, no attempt to assume regulated professional liability, and no code or dependency changes from this opportunity intake.
